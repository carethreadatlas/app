About CareThread Atlas

Health record timeline & clinical event explorer

Explore a personal health record timeline by category and chronology. CareThread Atlas keeps source fields and missing-record context within reach.

What this site does

Filter the timeline by clinical category, reverse the chronology, and expand any event to inspect its original resource fields.

A focused product

CareThread Atlas focuses on health record timeline & clinical event explorer. Its own interface, public documentation and source repository support the product’s continued development.

The connection implementation uses FinchNode for patient authorization and normalized records. The product concentrates on its own workflow, while a separate server handles the connection. Read the privacy notice for the current consent and data-handling details.

Current availability

Live record connections are awaiting production activation. Public guides are available now; deployment alone does not establish a completed patient connection.

Public information and private records

Guide search runs in your browser and covers public explanatory pages only. It does not access a patient session, index health records or send search terms to an analytics service. Opening the homepage or an article never starts a record import.

Read the privacy and data-handling notice before connecting. Medical questions belong with your care team; this site documents software behavior.

Source code and corrections

Inspect the public CareThread Atlas repository for implementation, license, setup and tests. For privacy questions or factual corrections, contact software@council.health. Please do not include private health records in a public GitHub issue.
